# Water/6-31G determinant gradient checks

Geometry (angstrom): `O 0 0 0; H .05 -.75 .59; H -.03 .78 .62`.
The asymmetric geometry avoids imposing equilibrium forces or point-group
zeros. Conventional molecular RHF, integrals and orbital response use CCpy.
RHF and CC/EOM equations are re-solved at every numerical displacement.

## Primary correlation model: frozen O(1s)

The molecule has ten electrons and 13 spatial orbitals. One occupied spatial
orbital is frozen in the correlation calculation: eight electrons are
correlated, and no basis orbitals are deleted. The full determinant support
has 245,025 determinants, including ranks beyond P/Q generated by the
excitation exponentials. This is a frozen-core calculation, not all-electron.

P contains all 64 singles, all 1,360 doubles, 2,480 active triples and 5,210
active quadruples (9,114 total). Active occupied MOs are 1–4 and active
virtual MOs are 5–9, with zero-based indices. Q contains every remaining
triple (8,720) and quadruple (37,490), a total of 46,210 determinants. Coverage
and disjointness are asserted exactly; Q is not sampled.

Ground and the lowest two projected excited roots in the fixed-Ms=0
determinant sector were checked, with MP and full denominators. These are
energy-ordered roots without an additional total-spin filter. Four-point
central differences use 0.001 bohr steps in H1/z and H2/y (atoms 1 and 2,
zero-based). All nine analytical Cartesian components are recorded, but
only those two components are directly finite differenced in this model.
Base, correction and total derivatives are compared separately.

All six cases pass the 2e-8 Eh/bohr tolerance. Errors below are maximum
absolute deviations in Eh/bohr over the two tested components.

## Separate reduced-correlation model: all Cartesian components

An additional, faster test freezes three occupied orbitals (six electrons),
correlating four electrons in the same 13-orbital basis. It has 2,025
determinants. P contains 32 singles, 312 doubles, 200 triples and 100
quadruples. Q contains the complementary 696 triples and 684 quadruples.
This reduced correlation model is separate from the primary O(1s)-frozen
calculation above.

All nine Cartesian components were directly finite differenced for all six
state/denominator cases. Errors below are maximum absolute deviations in
Eh/bohr over those nine components.

## Reproduction and implementation checks

Build the optional FCIpy connector using `devtools/build_fcipy_oracle.py` and
set `CCPY_FCIPY_EXTENSION` to its printed extension path. With the CCpy build
environment active:

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python examples/ccpq_complete_spaces.py \
  --geometry 'O 0 0 0; H .05 -.75 .59; H -.03 .78 .62' \
  --basis 6-31g --nfrozen 1 --models sdtq --states 0 1 2 \
  --active-occupied 1 2 3 4 --active-virtual 5 6 7 8 9 \
  --quadruple-stride 1 --components 1:2 2:1 --verify-roots \
  --output examples/results/h2o_631g_frozen_core.json --resume
```

For the reduced all-component demonstration, change `--nfrozen` to 3,
`--active-occupied` to `3 4`, and components to
`0:0 0:1 0:2 1:0 1:1 1:2 2:0 2:1 2:2`, with a separate output file.

Displaced MO signs are aligned through cross-geometry AO overlaps. Excited
roots are followed within an excitation-energy gap guard and a right-vector
overlap guard, with unchanged residual checks. Every Hamiltonian value is
recomputed from each geometry's integrals on a reusable Slater graph. The
all-electron water cache would require about 44.5 GB for values/indices and
exceeds the connector's current 32-bit entry-count capacity; it was not run.

The regression suite passed 135 tests (`tests/gradients` plus
`tests/scf/test_ccpq_gradient.py`), including independent dense checks of
Hamiltonian refresh, structural zero derivatives, and excited-root following.
The quick water regression explicitly uses the reduced three-frozen-orbital
model. After expanding it to all nine Cartesian components, its six cases
passed again in 65.21 seconds alongside the primary calculation. These
calculations test derivative consistency of the stated theory
and selections; they do not assess its physical energy accuracy.
